# Resetting your password

If you can't log in, use **Forgot password** on the login page. A reset link is sent to the email on your account and is valid for 60 minutes.

- Check spam/junk folders.
- If the email on your account is out of date, contact Member Services; we will verify your identity before changing it.
- After 5 failed attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes.

Member Services never asks for your password.
