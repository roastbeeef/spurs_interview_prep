# Membership renewals

- Memberships run from 1 July to 30 June.
- The renewal window closes on **30 June**. After that, the membership becomes **lapsed**.
- Lapsed members may request reinstatement until **31 December** of the same year. Reinstatement is reviewed by Member Services and is subject to availability; a seat is not guaranteed.
- Prices: Gold £150, Silver £75, Junior £25.

## Payment issues
If you believe you were charged incorrectly (for example, a duplicate payment), Member Services will investigate. Do not raise a chargeback with your bank while an investigation is open. Refunds for confirmed duplicate payments are processed within 10 working days.
