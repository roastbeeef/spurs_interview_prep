# Transferring a seat for a match

Members can transfer their seat for a single match to another person.

- The recipient **must have a registered account**.
- Recipients under 18 must be transferred a Junior-priced seat in an eligible area; the price difference is charged to the member.
- Transfers close 24 hours before kick-off.
- Transfers are done by the member in **My Account > Tickets > Transfer**. Member Services cannot transfer seats on a member's behalf.
